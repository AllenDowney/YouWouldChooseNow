# You Would Choose Now

In 2016, Barack Obama asked whether — if you did not know who you would be — you would choose to be born now rather than in an earlier decade. This book uses long-running survey data (mainly the GSS and ANES) to take that question seriously topic by topic: race, sexual orientation, gender, religion, and related themes.

## Notebooks

Teaching notebooks that walk through the period-only and period–cohort models used in the book. Open either link to run on Google Colab:

- [Period-only models](https://colab.research.google.com/github/AllenDowney/YouWouldChooseNow/blob/main/notebooks/time_model_appendix.ipynb) — national trend over survey years (linear logit → RW1 → RW2 → RW2 with overdispersion), using GSS attitudes toward same-sex relations
- [Period–cohort models](https://colab.research.google.com/github/AllenDowney/YouWouldChooseNow/blob/main/notebooks/period_cohort_model_appendix.ipynb) — additive period–cohort surface and decade-specific period trends on the same outcome
